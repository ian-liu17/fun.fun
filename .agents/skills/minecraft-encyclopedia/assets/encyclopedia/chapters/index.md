# MINE!!! — Minecraft Illustrated Encyclopedia

## 01. Master index & glossary

A place for every block, and a path back.

Hardness: resistance to breaking. Blast resistance: resistance to explosions.

Renewable: obtainable repeatedly without exhausting finite world generation; unknown here unless explicitly researched.
