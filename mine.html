MAKE SOMETHING

Learn to craft

Mine tree trunks → make planks → craft a table. Select the table in slot 5 and press Q / Place. Stand beside it to craft tools and a sword.

Drag to look. Double-click locks your mouse; Escape releases it for crafting.

Beyond the Overworld

Craft 10 obsidian at your table. Build the purple portal east of camp (22, 33), then walk through. Piglins, blazes, ghasts and magma cubes roam the Nether.

The End gate is northeast at 60, 20. Hunt Endermen for pearls and Nether blazes for rods. Craft powder and 12 eyes, then activate the gate. Bring a crystal bow (V fires) to destroy tower crystals and fight the dragon.

Obsidian crafting, automatic portal lighting, the End gate location and the crystal bow are simplified MINE!!! rules.
A LIVING WORLD

Meet your neighbours

Sheep, cows, pigs and chickens roam across the field. Zombies, slimes, skeletons and Endermen appear at night. Defeated night mobs are replaced elsewhere.

Find an animal in the meadow.

A full cycle lasts 6 minutes. First sunset: 3:00. First night: 3:30 of active play. Pause stops the clock.

    Your beacon expedition

    Move with WASD. Drag the world to look around. Double-click the world to lock the mouse; Escape releases it so you can use the crafting panel. Space jumps; Shift runs. Aim the crosshair at a nearby block and press E / left-click to mine. Q / right-click places your selected block. Use 1–7 or the hotbar to select materials. Arrow keys also move and turn; Page Up/Down looks up/down.

    On a phone, drag to look, use the arrow pad to move, and tap Mine / Place. Jump climbs one-block steps. The amber lanterns east of camp mark the cave entrance.

    Mine wood → craft planks → craft a table and sticks. Place the table (slot 5, Q). Nearby, craft a wooden pickaxe and sword. Mine stone, then craft a stone pickaxe and furnace. Hunt animals with left-click / Attack; H eats meat. Cook it at a furnace using a plank. Then mine three crystals underground. Craft a beacon and place it to win. Dig stairs, take care with long falls, and jump over the slimes! Falls, monster contact and skeleton arrows damage your hearts. An empty hunger bar slowly drains health. Full hunger regenerates hearts. Running out of hearts returns you to the surface with your inventory.

    Mobs: use Find a mob or press F to look toward an animal. Aim at it and press R to greet it. At night, zombies and slimes can chase and hurt you; Left-click attacks all mobs, including animals, which drop meat. R greets animals and attacks monsters. Animals never hurt you. Skeletons shoot arrows; Endermen hit harder. Mobs walk on the actual terrain and cannot pass through solid walls.

    Day and night: sunset begins after 3 minutes; night begins after 3 minutes 30 seconds of active play. Moonlight and stars appear, and the HUD counts down to sunrise. Press N or use the Wait button to skip ahead. Your clock and mobs are saved with your world.

    The original soundtrack shifts between meadow, nighttime and crystal-cave themes. Music pauses with the game and when the tab is hidden. Choose Survival or Creative in the sidebar. Creative has unlimited blocks, flight (Space up, C down), no hunger and no damage. This game uses simplified survival and its own beacon recipe. Nether portal: craft 10 obsidian, build it with the travel button, then walk into it east of camp. For the End, bring 12 eyes to the gate at 60, 20. Use a crystal bow (V / Bow) on the four tower crystals, then the dragon. The dragon lands in the center periodically and can also be hit with a sword. The return gate opens after victory.
