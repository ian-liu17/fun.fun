    Your beacon expedition

    Move with WASD. Use your mouse or drag the world to look around. Space jumps; Shift runs. Aim the crosshair at a nearby block and press E / left-click to mine. Q / right-click places your selected block. Use 1–5 or the hotbar to select materials. Arrow keys also move and turn; Page Up/Down looks up/down.

    On a phone, drag to look, use the arrow pad to move, and tap Mine / Place. Jump climbs one-block steps. The amber lanterns east of camp mark the cave entrance.

    Collect five wood, craft a pickaxe, and mine three crystals underground. Craft a beacon and place it to win. Dig stairs, take care with long falls, and jump over the slimes! A fall or slime can cost a heart. Running out of hearts returns you to the surface with your inventory.

    Mobs: use Find a mob or press F to look toward an animal. Aim at it and press R to greet it. At night, zombies and slimes can chase and hurt you; R or left-click attacks them. Animals never hurt you. Mobs walk on the actual terrain and cannot pass through solid walls.

    Day and night: sunset begins after 3 minutes; night begins after 3 minutes 30 seconds of active play. Moonlight and stars appear, and the HUD counts down to sunrise. Press N or use the Wait button to skip ahead. Your clock and mobs are saved with your world.

    The original soundtrack shifts between meadow, nighttime and crystal-cave themes. Music pauses with the game and when the tab is hidden. This game uses its own simplified recipes.
