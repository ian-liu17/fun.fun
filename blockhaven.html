WELCOME TO YOUR LITTLE WORLD

Explore. Collect. Make it yours.

Move and turn with WASD or the arrow keys, or click/tap open land to walk there. Drag with a mouse, touchpad, or finger to steer. Use the on-screen arrows on a phone. Choose Collect (E), then click a nearby tree, rock, flower, or ground block to collect it.

Choose an inventory item, switch to Build, then click a nearby tile to place it. You can stack blocks up to five high. Right-click also builds.

Your backpack has 20 slots. Each holds up to 24 identical items. A bundle takes one slot and holds 24 mixed items. Select a stack and click Pack; click an item inside a bundle to unpack it.

Craft planks, bricks, glass, lanterns, and more bundles. Your world saves on this browser. There are no enemies or timers—just a world to explore.
