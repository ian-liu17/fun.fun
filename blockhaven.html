Your world, in first person.

WASD moves forward, backward, and sideways. Arrow up/down moves; arrow left/right turns. Space jumps. Page Up/Down looks up/down.

Click the world for mouse or touchpad look. Esc frees your cursor. If mouse capture is unavailable, drag to look. On a touchscreen, drag the world to look and use the arrow pad to move.

Aim the crosshair at a block within 5 blocks. Press E to mine it. It breaks into a little cube—walk near it to pick it up. Select a building block and press Q or right-click to place it against the highlighted face. Solid blocks stop you walking through them.

Craft string from leaves, then a fishing rod from 3 wood and 2 string. Select the rod, aim at pond water, and press F to cast. Press F again when the bobber dips to catch a fish.
